Conclusions
2.10.2026 9
1. Europe needs a stronger, more integrated financial system to meet its 
triple test — defence, energy, productivity. Completing the Savings 
and Investments Union is central.
2. A well-designed European safe asset can support this agenda — if it 
protects financial stability, channels idle savings into productive 
investment, and safeguards sustainable public finances.
3. The ESRB can help advance this debate with sound, independent 
analysis of the financial-stability implications.
Europe has strengthened its institutions over 15 years of crises. 
The world keeps changing fast — our task is to keep the financial system 
resilient, and capable of financing the growth ahead.
