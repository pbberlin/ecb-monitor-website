### ECB Interest Rate Decision – “Price Stability Takes Priority Over Economic Stabilization”
High core inflation dampens rate cut euphoria
fhe-grey

As expected, the ECB Governing Council has decided to leave key interest rates unchanged. Friedrich Heinemann, Head of the Research Unit “Corporate Taxation and Public Finance” at ZEW Mannheim and Professor at Heidelberg University, comments:

Since the beginning of the year, the rate cut euphoria in the markets has experienced a noticeable setback. This is clearly visible in the marked recovery of German government bond yields. The view that interest rates could remain high for a longer period is gaining more supporters again. The reason for this—and for the ECB’s caution—is persistently high core inflation. A very important figure for the Governing Council’s further deliberations will be the January inflation rate. If inflation makes a significant jump upward due to the many price increases at the start of the year, the timing of a possible rate cut will be pushed back. Of course, the weak economic situation in the euro area would in principle argue in favor of a swift rate cut. However, the EU Treaty is clear in cases of such target conflicts: within the ECB’s objective framework, price stability takes precedence over economic stabilization.
