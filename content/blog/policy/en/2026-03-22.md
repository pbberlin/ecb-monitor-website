### Hawk or Dove in the Succession to Christine Lagarde at the Helm of the European Central Bank?
Insights from an AI Analysis of Public Speeches
fhe-jkp

The final term of Christine Lagarde ends in October 2027. According to press reports, she is considering stepping down earlier. Currently, four individuals are being discussed as possible candidates to succeed her at the head of the ECB: former Spanish central bank governor Pablo Hernández de Cos, former Dutch central bank chief Klaas Knot, current Bundesbank president Joachim Nagel, and German ECB Executive Board member Isabel Schnabel.

This policy brief examines how these four candidates can be classified in terms of monetary policy stance and whether they are more likely to be considered a "dove" or a "hawk." Methodologically, it relies on AI-supported analyses of public speeches.
