### Tom Bugdalle on How Kevin Warsh Might Alter Central Bank Research
The Fed is a big player in evaluating its own policies
bugdalle

The Fed doesn't just set interest rates, it dominates the research that evaluates them: 6% of global macro publications, 40% of central-bank citations, editorial control at top journals. Now, with Kevin Warsh as Fed Chair since May 2026 and openly critical of the institution's "groupthink," the question is whether this self-reinforcing dominance in monetary policy research will change.
