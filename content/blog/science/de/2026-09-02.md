### Ulrike Malmendier and Stefan Nagel on the Risks that Inflation Expectations May Lose Their Anchor
Inflation expectations and personal lifetime experience
malmendier-nagel

Stable long-run inflation expectations during the post-COVID surge look like proof that inflation targets have anchored public beliefs. Malmendier and Nagel argue in their new NBER paper that this is an illusion: people form expectations from their own lifetime experience of inflation, not from announced targets, and decades of low inflation simply made expectations look anchored. Their warning: another sustained inflation episode would de-anchor expectations again, target or no target. Central banks can't talk their way to credibility, only a sustained record of low realized inflation earns it.
