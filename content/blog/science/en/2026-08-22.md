### John H. Cochrane, Luis Garicano and Klaus Masuch on Necessary Reforms to Protect ECB Independence
A watchdog for the ECB and a framework for sovereign debt restructuring 
cochrane-garicano-masuch

The euro area's fiscal safety net has a hole: broken fiscal rules leave states dangerously little room to borrow, banks keep loading up on their own governments' debt, and the ECB's crisis interventions have quietly killed market discipline. Cochrane, Garicano and Masuch propose the fix: an independent European Fiscal Institution, banking reform to break the sovereign-bank doom loop, and orderly sovereign debt restructuring, with the ECB stepping back.
