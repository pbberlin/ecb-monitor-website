### Manoj Pradhan and Charles Goodhart on the new constraints of central bankers with ageing populations
Secular trends weaken central banks
goodhart-pradhan

Pradhan and Goodhart argue that central banks are losing the capacity to fight inflation single-mindedly — not through their own failings, but because the macro environment has shifted beneath them. Ageing societies will erode public finances far more than official projections admit, and government dissaving will outweigh rising household savings, lifting real interest rates. China's disinflationary contribution fades over the medium term, and AI is unlikely to compensate. The policy implication is uncomfortable and points in the direction of fiscal dominance. Disinflating heavily indebted economies risks financial instability now, or, ironically, higher inflation later.
