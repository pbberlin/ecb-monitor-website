To set up this specific two-stage, multi-hire contest on Upwork, follow these mechanical steps:

1. Account Type & Setup
Account Type: A standard, free Upwork Client Account is all you need. You do not need an Enterprise or special account.
Payment Setup: Upwork operates in USD. Your €700 budget is roughly **760USD∗∗.

Set your budget values in USD equivalent (110 USD for Stage 1, $440 USD for Stage 2).

2. Job Post Settings

* Job Type:                Choose Fixed-Price
* Budget:                  Set the main job budget to the Stage 1 price ($110 USD / ~€100). 
                            Explain in the text that this is the entry price for 3 selected pool participants, 
                            with a $440 / ~€400 expansion for the winner.

* Number of Freelancers:   Select "Many freelancers" (this allows you to hire 3 people from a single job posting).


3. How to Execute the Two Stages (The Workflow)

Execute this manually using Milestones:

Stage 1 (Hiring the 3 Candidates):
 * Review applicants to your post.
 * Click "Hire" on the 3 best candidates. This creates 3 separate, active contracts.
 * Set the contract terms for each to: Fixed Price, $110 USD, Milestone 1: "Produce 2 conceptual drafts."
 * Escrow will require you to deposit 110foreachcandidate(330 total deposited).
 * Once they submit the drafts, click "Approve and Release" to pay all three.
 * Close the contracts of the two runners-up and leave them honest feedback.

Stage 2 (Hiring the Winner):
 * Go to the winning freelancer’s active contract.
 * Click "Add Milestone".
 * Name it "Stage 2: 6 remaining illustrations" and set the value to $440 USD (~€400).
 * Funded escrow will hold the $440.
 * Once the final work is approved, release the milestone and close the contract.

4. Bidding & Filtering Settings to Choose

To protect your budget and filter out low-quality/AI bids:

Candidate Requirements:

 * Set English Level to: Fluent or Bilingual.
 
 * Set Job Success Score (JSS) to: 90% and above (prevents scammers).
 
 * Screening Questions (Crucial for filtering AI): Add these custom questions to the post:
 
        * "Do you use generative AI at any stage of your design process? (Be honest, we check vector paths)."
 
        * "Can you deliver final source files in SVG format or an SVGator project file?"
 
 * Bid Type: Allow Standard Bidding. Do not use "Estimated hours." Encourage bidders to bid exactly the $110 Stage 1 price.
