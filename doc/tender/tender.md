# Eight illustrations for macroeconomics website

We need an illustrator / graphics designer with a firm grasp of macroeconomics.

You should be a talented illustrator _and_ have knowledge of macroeconomic concepts. 

Illustrating macroeconomic concepts is challenging - and not very rewarding - 
if you have no serious interest in economics, this offer is not worth your time.

## Who we are

We are the [ZEW Institute](https://www.zew.de/en/), a German economic research institute, mostly state funded.

We do research for the benefit of Europe and society as a whole.

You will compete to do a contract work for the department [Public Finance](https://www.zew.de/en/research-at-zew/corporate-taxation-and-public-finance).

Here are the [people](https://www.zew.de/en/research-at-zew/corporate-taxation-and-public-finance/staff-contact) you will do the work for.

You will work with [Peter Buchmann](https://www.zew.de/en/team/pbu) and [Jan Kemper](https://www.zew.de/en/team/jkp). Decision of the winning bid will also include [Prof. Heinemann](https://www.zew.de/en/team/fhe).


## Tender and payment

The tender is competitive and has two stages.

First stage: You produce two conceptual illustrations that indicate how you would fulfill the complete contract.

There will be two weeks time for this stage.

The best three applications will each receive a €50 thank-you payment.

Then we will choose the best bid among those leading three. The best bid will be awarded the full contract of 400€.

The full contract consists of EIGHT distinct images / illustrations. You can pick two color schemes of your choice. The most important images are the symbols for "dovish" and "hawkish" monetary policy. Details below. Of those two images, we need a simplified version for the legend bottom right. For the two competitive illustrations, at least one should be for "dovish" or "hawkish". You have three weeks time.

Additional reward: Your name and picture will be listed at third position at the "team" page.  
We will write a two sentence statement - explaining and recommending your contribution.
Your name will be linked to your personal online portfolio as quality reference for you for other clients.
(your portfolio website cannot contain indecent stuff of course).

Your productions will also be listed on the "image credits" page.

The website will be published in September 2026 under 
<https://ecb-monitor.zew.de>

Even now, the pre-release data received wide media attention.
For instance the data from [screen 02c](#screens-02c) was printed in two-column size in the leading German "Handelsblatt" newspaper.  
Chances are, your work will be seen by many media people and financial professionals.



You must give us full exclusive copyright buyout though.
Your works will only be used for the aforementioned project.
As we are a research institute, we will not use your works for any commercial purpose.

Payment will be made within 10 business days.

We would prefer payment via Paypal or SWIFT bank transfer.
Make sure you have a Paypal account - or a bank account that is capable of receiving money via SWIFT.



### Africa, Asia, South America

While our work has a focus on Europe,  
we welcome people from other continents and different backgrounds.

You live in a small village in Brazil?  You are from Bangalore in India?  

You only need Internet access, English language and some talent in graphics and economics.

## Task

Improve existing graphics and symbol pictures for several web pages.

The section [Website Screens](#website-screens) contains all major frames of the existing website.

All screens have a second version `..._redesign.jpg`. The color magenta shows those areas that need to be re-created.

The other areas should not be changed - unless you have a compelling aesthetic vision.  
Keep in mind, that the website is a sub-site of our main website https://www.zew.de and should not deviate too much from our CI.


### First part - Home page  

[Screen 01](#screen-01) defines the work to be done.

Define the first color scheme.  You can stick with the existing "iceblue" and "zew green", or choose two other colors from section [images from ZEW institute](#zew-charts).

Then design the six section graphics.

You can use ideas (but you cannot copy anything) from the Economist magazine.

<https://www.economist.com/topics/finance-and-economics>

<https://www.economist.com/topics/free-exchange>

<https://www.economist.com/schools-brief>

<https://www.economist.com/topics/graphic-detail>

The graphics do not need to be super fine-grained - rough shapes that can be easily recognized are preferred.

Again: Look at the examples in at the Economist website.

The most important challenge here is to find illustrations for the actual content of the sections. 

For instance: The second section should easily convey the topic "Inside the brains of ECB directors" or "Inside the mechanism of the ECB" - or "credit expansion and contraction". Do not use commonplace symbols like the percentage sign to symbolize interest rates - or the Euro symbol to symbolize "money supply". We make this tender to get creative yet intuitive illustrations. 

Do not try to trace or copy *The Economist* illustrations - this is easily detectable and will disqualify you.


### Second part - color scheme "dovish" - "hawkish".  

Define the second color scheme.  

Screens [02a](#screens-02a), [02b](#screens-02b) and [02c](#screens-02c) all feature a gradient between two anchor colors.

First color is currently violet-blue and should be associated with "liquidity", "dovish monetary policy", "low interest rates", "easy credit", "expansion", "inflation".  

Second is currently yellow-orange. It should be associated with "hawkish monetary policy", "scarce liquidity", "higher interest rates", "less money available", "price stability", "anti-inflation".

* You can keep the existing colors. 

* You can also define _your_ colors.

* You may also use our ZEW colors (folder `inspiration-zew-colors`) - if you can find a semantically suitable mapping for "dovish" and "hawkish".  
    The section [images from ZEW institute](#zew-charts) contains some example graphics using the ZEW color scheme.

* Dont use "value judgement" colors: "red" and "green" would imply that one is good - and the other is bad.  
Neither "dovish" nor "hawkish" positions are "good" or "bad"..



#### Color Saturation

Please create the second part graphics in _fully_ saturated colors.

The web pages have an "opacity" setting (visible bottom right) so users can make the colors more or less opaque / garish.

If a user visits the page the first time, the opacity of all graphics is set to 50 percent.

Thus, produce the graphics in full intensity colors (this looks really intense - I know), since they will be "de-saturated" in the design of the website.

Exception: Home page graphics - these will be displayed exactly as you produce them - here a moderate, modest color saturation is probably best.


## No AI

The draft images given were created with AI.

Please dont use any AI in creating the production images.
Please dont use AI for any small details either - 
as its shapes and gradients have salient aesthetics and characteristics that destroy any creative value.


## Format

SVG or Vector graphics welcome.

PNG or any other pixel format also nice, sufficient resolution 1600 x 1600 pixel or higher
